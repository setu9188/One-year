Seven things I love about you

Reasons you are my favourite person

Reason 1

Your smile

It is so cute that I forget what I was saying. Every time you smile at me, a boring day turns into a good one.

Reason 2

Your silly jokes

Nobody makes me laugh at things that are not even funny the way you do. Then you laugh at your own joke and I laugh even harder.

Reason 3

You always help me

Whenever I need help, you are there, usually before I finish asking. You make my problems feel smaller and me feel less alone with them.

Reason 4

Your voice

I could listen to you talk about anything. You explaining something with full energy, or just telling me about your day. It is my favourite sound in the world.

Reason 5

Random hugs and kisses

The ones that come out of nowhere are the best. A sudden hug in the middle of a long day can fix the whole day.

Reason 6

The lunch you bring me

You bring me lunch in college. It is the simplest, most practical kind of love, and it makes my whole day. I will never say no to it.

Reason 7

You make me try new things

New places, new food, things I would never have tried alone. I would have stayed in my comfort zone forever. You push me a little, and I end up loving it every time. Thank you for that.

One more thing

Thank you for this year

Tingi, a year ago I did not know how much of my day would end up being about you. Now the best part of almost every day is telling you about it, or just sitting next to you while nothing happens.

Thank you for the laughs, the help, the lunches, the hugs, and for pushing me to do things I would never do alone. I love you, and I am really happy it is you.

Yours, always. Setu
